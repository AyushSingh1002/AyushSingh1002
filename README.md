A full-stack gym management platform that centralizes member, membership, payment, attendance, and operations management.

**What I built:**

- Role-based access control for **Admin, Staff, and Member** roles
- Secure JWT-based authentication
- Full membership lifecycle: plan creation, renewals, expiry alerts, freeze & cancellation workflows
- Razorpay integration with a manual payment fallback
- Automated invoicing and payment reconciliation
- Financial and operational dashboards with real-time reporting
- Staff activity tracking
